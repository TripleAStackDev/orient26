Core Study Resources

The following table provides links to important study documents, explains their contents and describes how they relate to my studies.

Resource What It Contains Personal Relevance
Curriculum Information about the degree structure, courses, credits, course timing and learning outcomes. This helps me understand what to expect from my courses, plan my semesters and follow my progress towards graduation.
Academic Calendar Information about academic periods, semester dates, holidays and important dates in the academic year. This helps me organise my schedule and keep track of important academic dates. I can also use it to find out when contact teaching begins in the next academic year.
Study Policy Degree regulations and additional guidelines concerning students' rights, responsibilities, assessment and study procedures, including applicable rules for retakes and grade improvement. Understanding these regulations helps me know what is expected of me and what options are available if I need to improve a course result.
Cheating and Plagiarism Information about academic integrity, misconduct, investigation procedures and possible disciplinary consequences. This helps me understand the seriousness of academic misconduct. In programming assignments, I must submit honest work, acknowledge sources where required and follow the instructions for each task.
Thesis Guidance on the thesis process, supervision, maturity tests, the Wihi tool and thesis submission. Although the thesis is still ahead of me, understanding the process early gives me an idea of the work required to complete my degree.
Practical Training Information about practical training requirements, procedures, employer agreements, reporting and the registration of credits. This is relevant to my career because practical training can help me apply software development skills in a professional environment and gain valuable work experience.
Credit Transfer Information about applying for recognition of previous studies or other eligible learning through the applicable credit recognition procedures. I do not currently expect to need credit transfer because I am beginning my degree without previous related higher education studies. However, it is useful to know that this option exists.
Cross-Institutional Studies Information about studying courses offered by other higher education institutions through available cooperation arrangements and registration procedures. I may not need this immediately, but it could allow me to broaden my knowledge by taking relevant courses outside my own programme.
